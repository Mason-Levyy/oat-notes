# Bundled speaker-recognition model

`3dspeaker_speech_eres2net_sv_en_voxceleb_16k.onnx` is an ERes2Net speaker
verification model from the ModelScope 3D-Speaker project, distributed under
the Apache License 2.0. The model is used entirely on-device to turn a speech
segment into a compact embedding.
